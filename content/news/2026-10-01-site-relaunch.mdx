The blog moved from `blog-tree.github.io` to **zagovorichev.github.io** and was rebuilt from scratch:

- **Guides** — my step-by-step instructions for problems I have solved.
- **Videos** — talks and tutorials worth watching, with my notes.
- **Links** — websites I find genuinely useful and what I found there.
- **News** — short notes about things I follow.

Under the hood it is Next.js with MDX, published to GitHub Pages by GitHub Actions on every commit.
There is also an [RSS feed](/feed.xml).
